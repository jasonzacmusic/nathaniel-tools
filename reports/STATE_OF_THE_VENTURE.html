Audited Multiple project tabs, multiple tracks — the design holds

Read the apps specifically for the multi-tab question. Palette & Look re-reads the current tab every frame and re-syncs within a quarter second of any change, so switching tabs can never show stale tracks. Track Settings Transfer keeps a separate identity map per open project — cross-session transfer is its whole job and the bookkeeping is per-project. The safety spine stores track identities that survive delete, undo, reorder and tab switches.

Two honest gaps: this laptop's REAPER (7.28) doesn't yet have ReaImGui installed, so the three windowed apps can't open here until it's added via ReaPack — and the live in-REAPER test run on this laptop is the next engineering session. The static audit found no multi-tab defects.

Built The concert decoder — your MainStage rig is now readable

New tool in the repo reads any of your 100+ .concert files and prints the whole rig: setlist, every patch, every channel strip, every instrument — and which sounds can or can't load in REAPER. Ran it on Lagori 2026 Minimal (your newest, 21 July): 11 song patches plus the full covers set inside Darbari — Aasma, Humma Humma G→A, Param Sundari, Kesariya, Vaathi, Jhumka, Uptown Funk and more, 57 patches in all.

This was the handoff's "suggested first deliverable" for StageRig — the one-day job that tells us whether the whole idea is viable. It's done, and the answer is yes.

Planned StageRig v0 — five patches from your real set

Aasma · Humma Humma G→A (the horns patch) · BRAHMA Gm · Upright · Maari Kannu. The two you named, plus three that between them exercise every substitution problem. v0 is done when you can switch between them blind and not tell the switching apart from MainStage. Full spec is in the repo — including the Ableton-style Loop Deck (loops rendered from your own sessions at show tempo, launched quantized to the bar, everything pre-loaded, one PANIC pad) and the exact on-stage REAPER settings profile that makes it trustworthy.

Market check (fresh research, in the repo): MainStage is visibly neglected, REAPER's existing live tools are dead or unproven, and the paid rivals charge $149–199. Nobody owns "MainStage-grade switching + Ableton-grade loop trust on REAPER". The existing NSM Bridge stays as-is — StageRig doesn't need it.

Planned The business: give the tools away, the SWS way

Researched how SWS, ReaPack, ReaTeam, X-Raym, ReaperTips and The REAPER Blog actually work in 2026. The model: the suite is free forever — one-click install via our own ReaPack repository, flagship scripts submitted to ReaTeam (which is built into every REAPER user's ReaPack by default — the single biggest reach lever, and it costs one PR). The money lives in the course and, later, StageRig Pro ($79–129 anchor under Gig Performer's $169 umbrella). X-Raym proved this exact shape: 626 free scripts feeding a paid shop. Full plan committed to the repo.
