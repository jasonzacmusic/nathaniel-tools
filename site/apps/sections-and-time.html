Sections & Time

One window for the things that decide where you are in a song: markers, regions, the tempo map, and the timebase setting that quietly makes all three behave differently than you expect.

In development Designed, not built Green · structure
Status, stated plainly

This app does not exist yet. The design is settled and the four scripts it will absorb are shipping today as part of the speed layer. Nothing on this page is a promise about a date.

The problem

The marker looks like it's on the bar. It isn't. And I can't tell by looking.

The class of bug this app is aimed at

Markers, regions and tempo markers all sit on the timeline, and all three can land a few milliseconds off a bar line without any visible sign. Zoom out and everything looks aligned. Zoom in three months later, when you are cutting a lesson to the grid, and it is not.

Three separate mechanisms cause it, and they are all invisible from the arrange view:

  • Snapping obeys the arrange grid, not the bar. With a grid division of 0.125, a marker snap lands on the nearest eighth note. That is usually not where the bar is.
  • Tempo markers insert at the raw edit cursor. Action 40256 does not quantize. Press it twice and you have two markers milliseconds apart, both looking like they are on the same bar, fighting each other.
  • The project timebase is not what you think. itemtimelock = 0 means the project is in Time, not Beats — so items do not follow tempo changes. There is no obvious place in REAPER's UI where a per-track answer to that question is visible.

What exists today

The hard parts are already written and already shipping as keyboard scripts. This app is the window around them, not new arithmetic:

  • Marker at BarTrue measure boundary from TimeMap_GetMeasureInfo — integer measures, no float drift. Shipping.
  • Tempo at BarInsert or edit a tempo and time-signature marker welded to the bar line. Shipping.
  • Region Next / PreviousMove between regions without flipping a global preference. Shipping.

What is designed

  • One windowMarkers, regions and tempo markers in one editable list instead of three unrelated dialogs.
  • Named section typesThe naming convention already used across eighty riff and lesson projects — C202, V218, V127.3 — as first-class types rather than free text you have to spell right.
  • Timebase viewA per-track timebase column, so the itemtimelock trap is a thing you can see rather than a thing you discover.
  • Tempo map hygieneFind near-duplicate tempo markers that look like one marker, and collapse them onto the bar line.
  • Bar-locked by defaultEverything the app writes is quantized to the measure unless you explicitly ask otherwise.

What is not decided

Whether the section list should be able to move audio and MIDI along with a section boundary, or only describe the timeline. Moving material is far more useful and considerably more dangerous, and it is not a decision worth making before the describing half has been used in real sessions for a while.

Install

There is nothing to install for this app yet. The three scripts above arrive with the rest of the suite: 

https://github.com/jasonzacmusic/nathaniel-tools/raw/main/index.xml

Marker at Bar, Tempo at Bar and Region Next/Previous appear in the action list under Nathaniel Tools as soon as the repository is imported.
